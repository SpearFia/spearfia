# Initial treatment catalog

Draft for creator audition. Broad explanatory patterns were studied in the supplied Glooby reference; no graphics, audio or exact motion curves were copied. The manually supplied Gemini observations were sampled at 1 fps and do not establish precise sound timing. This pilot's timing comes from its own narration observations and animation frames.

| Family | Use when | Avoid when | First audition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Progressive explanation | 2–4 spoken points benefit from accumulating inside one container | The list is too fast to read, or footage demonstrates the point better | Time → ammunition → strength |
| Paired comparison and focus handoff | Two short statements make a meaningful distinction | The comparison invents an equivalence or conceals needed visual evidence | Fight won → still wanted |
| Source/context card | A brief quotation or summary benefits from visible provenance | The source is absent or a summary would be mistaken for independent proof | Pauses, help and changes of situation; explicitly labeled narration summary |

One reusable component takes supplied words, reveal frames and provenance. It does not write claims, select unverified footage or infer that an event occurred. The look uses the existing narration's tone: dark slate, ivory, muted gold and a limited red emphasis. Rounded cards preserve spatial continuity. New information enters in narration order; the second comparison item takes focus while the first stays readable. The bottom area is reserved for captions.

Sound belongs to an action. A short pop marks new information; a whoosh follows movement; an impact marks the meaningful change in the comparison. The sound's measured transient is aligned to the visual target, rather than placing the beginning of every file on a cut. Music remains low beneath the voice. Overlapping sound tails must survive export. The restrained and expressive auditions vary motion damping and effect gain while keeping the information and narration identical.

Clef receives the candidate treatment, plain footage, and none-of-these, with nearby narration observations and neighboring treatments. Its response must include every option's probability and the unchanged context hash. A provisional 0.95 probability and 0.20 lead are recorded for later evaluation, but every result is shadow-only. Test against creator-labeled examples before enabling any automatic selection; a confident unsupported answer is still an error. A writing model handles labels and bespoke adaptations, code handles frame calculations and rendering, and the creator reviews the final picture and sound.

First approval should answer: does each graphic clarify its beat, can the labels be read at phone size, do the cues sound useful under the narration, and which motion/sound strength fits the channel? Save the approved treatment version and sound hashes before reuse. This prototype writes no approval records. Complex 3D diagrams, tracked object changes, automatic footage verification, live Clef inference and production project integration are deferred.
